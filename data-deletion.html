Data Deletion Instructions

For Flux Runner, Ember Forge and Hyper Olympic Clicker. Last updated 2 August 2026.

The only thing these games store is your progress — best score, currency, unlocked items, and how far through the game you are. There is no account to close. Here is how to remove it.

On Facebook or Messenger

Removing the game from your Facebook account deletes the saved progress held by Facebook on the game's behalf:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Choose Apps and Websites.
  3. Find the game in the list and select Remove.
  4. Tick the option to delete the posts and activity associated with it, if offered, then confirm.

Your progress is stored against an opaque player ID that Facebook scopes to this game. Once the game is removed, that saved data is no longer accessible and is deleted by Facebook in the course of removing the app.

In your browser

A copy of your progress is also kept locally on whichever device you played on. To clear it, delete site data for the page you played on — in most browsers this is Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data. Clearing it removes the local save on that device only.

Doing both — removing the app on Facebook and clearing site data on each device you played on — removes every copy of your game progress.

By request

If you would rather we handled it, or you want to confirm nothing remains, email zubeid.hendricks@gmail.com from the account you played on, or tell us the display name you used. We will action it and confirm back within 30 days.

What we cannot delete

We do not hold any data about you beyond the game progress described above, so there is nothing else on our side to remove. Ads shown inside the game are served by the host platform — Meta or CrazyGames — and any data they hold is governed by their own policies and deleted through their own controls.
